## Subject

Who: a visitor who came for a part or a tool and is about to click through to a shop, and the reviewer at an affiliate network who opens the footer to verify the site discloses itself. The visitor's job on these screens is nearly nil; they should never be in the way. The reviewer's job is to find the three pages in one move and read plain, unbroken text. What each needs first: the footer links must be findable at the bottom of any page; the demo notice must be seen before a price is trusted; a legal page must read like a document, not like a catalogue.

Existing screens looked at: product page at 390 px and category page at 1280 px (screenshots taken from the running stack, 2026-10-21). Header: site name left, border edge to edge, content column 64rem with 1.5rem sides. Page shell `.page`. Plain, one typeface, no shadows. The new pieces follow that.

## Flow

1. Any page → footer, bottom of the page, three links.
2. Footer link → one of three legal pages. Each is a dead end by design: header site name returns home, footer is the same.
3. On a legal page the reader scrolls; the only outbound links are the contact email (mailto) and www.aepd.es (privacy page only). Both open in the same tab (no new-tab behaviour, as for shop links).
4. Demo notice: no interaction; it appears on product and category pages when the page lists only demo offers.

Proposed routes (tech lead owns them): `/aviso-legal` (Aviso legal), `/privacidad` (Política de privacidad), `/como-nos-financiamos` (Cómo se financia {siteName}). The footer links point at these.

No cookie banner, no consent UI, anywhere (decision 0009, guardrail 5).

## Legal page layout

One layout serves all three pages. The page is `<main className="page">`, so header, padding and the footer are the same as elsewhere. Inside it, the text sits in a reading column.

Mapping legal-es.md to elements:

| In legal-es.md | Renders as |
|---|---|
| the first `###` under each `## Página: …` | the page `<h1>` (one per page) |
| every later `###` | `<h2>` |
| a paragraph | `<p>` |
| a `-` list | `<ul>` with bullets |
| a `**…**` lead-in at the start of a list item | `<strong>` around the lead-in words, including its closing full stop; the markers themselves never appear |
| a list item's continuation lines | the same item; line breaks in the source are not line breaks on the page |

Type and spacing, all existing tokens:
- h1: `--text-xl`, weight 700, line-height 1.2, `--color-text` (the Page title role). Margin below `--space-2`.
- h2: `--text-lg`, weight 700, `--color-text` (the Section title role). Margin `--space-3` above, `--space-1` below.
- Body `<p>` and `<li>`: `--text-md`, weight 400, line-height 1.5, colour `--color-text`. This departs from `.page p` (muted #555): on these pages the text is the content, not a note, so it takes the full text colour (17.4:1). The build must beat the specificity of `.page p`.
- `<p>`: margin `0 0 var(--space-2)`. `<ul>`: bullets (disc), left padding `--space-3`, margin `0 0 var(--space-2)`; `<li>` margin-bottom `--space-1`.
- Reading column: `max-width: 42rem`, left aligned in the 64rem page column (not centred), so the left edge lines up with the header site name and the footer. 42rem is a constant of the legal text component, like the 18rem of the group list, not a global token. At phone width the column is the 342 px content width.
- Long values: `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the column, so a long address, registry line or email wraps and never causes horizontal scroll.
- No panels, no boxes, no dividers between sections, no table of contents, no "last updated" line (the owner text has none).

Links inside the text (the contact email in three places, www.aepd.es once):
- Colour `--color-link`, **underlined at rest** (in a paragraph a link colour alone is under 3:1 against the text colour, so the underline is the cue), underline stays on hover, `:focus-visible` shows `--focus-ring` with offset 2px.
- The contact email: the address value is the link text and `mailto:` is its target. The words around it ("Correo electrónico: ", "Puedes contactar en ", "escribiendo a ") are not part of the link.
- www.aepd.es: the text `www.aepd.es` is the link text, target `https://www.aepd.es`. The parentheses and the full stop stay outside the link. Same tab, no new-tab behaviour.
- Inline links are exempt from the 44 px tap rule (they sit inside running text); the list-item line height still gives a comfortable target.

The "Datos registrales" line: it is the fifth item of the Aviso legal list. When OWNER_REGISTRY is blank (unset, empty, or only spaces), the whole `<li>` is absent: no "Datos registrales:" label, no empty bullet, no placeholder text, no gap. The list then has four items. When it holds a value, the item reads "Datos registrales: <value>" in the fifth place. No other line is ever omitted.

## Demo notice

Text: legal-es.md § Aviso de demostración (T9.3.3), the sentence under "Shown on pages that list only demo offers." Word for word. The English line "Shown on pages…" is an instruction to us, not copy, and must never appear on the page. No placeholder, no link, no heading, no extra label.

Where: the first element inside `<main>` of the product page and the category page, above the h1. Same place on both. It is not shown anywhere else: not on home, not on the empty category, not on the 404, not on the legal pages.

When it shows: only when the page lists at least one offer and every offer it lists is from a demo feed.
- Product page: every offer in the "Dónde comprarlo" list is demo. A product page with no offers shows no notice.
- Category page: every offer behind the cards on the displayed page is demo. A category with no products (empty state) shows no notice.
- A page with at least one real offer shows no notice (owner ruling: "only demo offers").

Treatment: one quiet outlined box, not a filled panel (system.md principle 4: the one filled panel is the brand panel on home).
- Box: `--color-bg` background, `1px solid var(--color-border)`, then a left rule `0.25rem solid var(--color-link)` (the rule is 6.9:1 against white, so it passes the 3:1 control rule; the sentence itself carries the meaning, the rule is not the only cue). Radius `--radius`. Padding `--space-2`. Margin below `--space-3`.
- Text: `--text-md`, weight 400, line-height 1.4, colour `--color-text` (not muted; this is a warning-grade sentence). The build must beat `.page p`.
- One element, `role="note"`, no icon, no close button, no dismissal (so no state to remember and no cookie).
- Width: the full width of the page column on both pages; on desktop the sentence fits on one or two lines at 64rem.

## Data needs

For the tech lead, who owns the contract. Each need says what the screen shows and what happens when it is absent.

- `siteName`: existing. Used in the Financing h1 and in the intro paragraphs of all three pages.
- Owner name, NIF, postal address, contact email: plain single-line strings, shown as text (the email also as a `mailto:` link). Needed by Aviso legal (all four) and Privacy (all four, in Responsable del tratamiento; the email again in Tus derechos). Absence: the screen must never show `{ownerName}` or a label with an empty value. UX recommends the same loud failure as a blank `SITE_NAME`, because an Aviso legal with no owner is a legal defect, not a cosmetic one; the tech lead decides.
- Owner registry: optional plain string. Absent, empty or whitespace only means the Datos registrales item is not rendered. This is the only value that may disappear.
- Values are plain text, never HTML; the screen escapes them. The email value is trimmed before use as a link target.
- Demo-only flag, product page: true when the page lists at least one offer and every listed offer is from a demo feed. False otherwise, including a product with no offers.
- Demo-only flag, category page: true when the displayed cards have at least one offer behind them and every offer behind the displayed cards is from a demo feed. False for an empty category. If the whole-category answer is cheaper to read, UX accepts it; it differs only on a mixed category.
- The flag is a plain yes/no on each page's data, never missing: a missing flag must not silently mean "real". The frontend should have no way to read a demo offer's identity apart from this flag.
- Footer: no data; three labels and three paths.
- Hidden or unapproved offers (guardrail 1) must not count toward the flag: the rule above is about the offers the page lists.
