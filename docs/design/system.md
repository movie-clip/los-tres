# Design system

Direction approved by the human on 2026-10-27 (S11.1, #242: "Q1 - Yes" on the direction, amber chosen by the designer, "ignore shadows and too complex effects for now"). The S5.1 and S5.2 system is the base. The tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` as CSS custom properties; where this document and that file differ, the file is the truth.

## Principles

1. **Scan, then click.** The visitor knows what they want. Every list reads in one fixed order, in the same place on every row: what it is, then what it costs (or how much is there), then where it goes.
2. **The price is the truth.** Prices are final, IVA included, and a page that shows prices says so once, near the top. No shipping, no totals. The price is the boldest type in its card or row and carries no colour of its own: a coloured price reads as a discount, and the site shows none.
3. **Plain beats clever.** One typeface, one accent, one ink, no shadows, no motion. Trust comes from consistency.
4. **The brand is the frame; the list is calm.** The bold elements are the ink frame (header, home masthead, footer) and the amber primary button (the shop link, and the search button on the home page). Nothing inside a list competes with the price.

## Spacing, shape, layout

- Spacing: `--space-0` 0.25rem, `--space-1` 0.5rem, `--space-2` 1rem, `--space-3` 1.5rem, `--space-4` 3rem. Fluid values are tokens: `--gutter` (page side padding, 1rem up to 1.5rem), `--page-pad-block` (`.page` top and bottom, 1.5rem up to 2.5rem), `--card-padding` (1rem up to 1.5rem), `--grid-gap` (1rem up to 1.5rem). They are `clamp()` values, so layout needs no media query for them.
- Radius: `--radius-lg` (0.75rem) on cards, panels and sheets; `--radius-md` (0.5rem) on buttons, tiles, fields and the placeholder; `--radius-pill` on chips; `--radius-sm` (0.25rem) is used by the best-price tag; it is still unused elsewhere in public components. `--radius` is an alias of `--radius-md` so admin files compile unchanged. Admin buttons and text fields stay square (their files set `border-radius: 0`).
- Page shell: `.page`, max width `--content-width` (64rem), `--page-pad-block` top and bottom, `--gutter` on the sides. Phone content is 358 px wide at 390; desktop content is 976 px wide at 1280.
- Header, masthead and footer share the column: their inner element uses the same max width and `--gutter`, so the site name lines up with the page content at every width (x = 16 at 390, x = 152 at 1280).
- Canvas and cards: the `body` is `--color-canvas`; cards, panels and sheets are `--color-bg` with a 1px `--color-border`. Cards stand off the canvas by fill and border only.
- Card grid: below 40rem one column; from 40rem `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(16rem, 100%), 1fr))`, gap `--grid-gap`: one column at 390, two at 768, three at 1280.
- Tile grid (home): one column below 40rem; from 40rem `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(9.5rem, 100%), 1fr))`, gap `--grid-gap`, each tile capped at 22rem. `auto-fit` drops empty tracks, so four tiles fill the row in four equal columns. The 9.5rem minimum, the 22rem cap and the 4rem and 5rem drawing boxes are constants of the tiles component, not global tokens.
- Product page: the breadcrumb, the hero, the offer panel and the description card sit in one column at every width, `--grid-gap` apart. From 52rem the hero is three columns (image, text, a 15rem price column set off by a left edge); below it the hero is the image and text on top and the price group under a hairline. The two-column layout (`minmax(0, 1fr)` and `minmax(0, 26rem)`) is removed.
- Media queries: public layout uses four. Pagination has one at `max-width: 24rem` (its grid fallback), the product page uses 52rem (hero columns), 40rem (offer row on one line) and 14rem (head stacks, detail terms stack above their values, the offer row becomes one column), all values already used elsewhere and no new value, and the home category tiles have two: `min-width: 40rem` (row becomes a vertical card) and `max-width: 14rem` (a phone at 200% zoom: drawing stacked above the name, count and chevron on one row, the count may wrap). The ranges never overlap. The category product card and grid add no new value: they reuse the tiles' 40rem and 14rem. The admin components have two more at `max-width: 40rem`. Everything else is fluid `clamp()` and `flex-wrap`.
- No shadow: `box-shadow` is not used and no token exists for it. No motion: no `transition`, `transform` or `animation`; states change instantly and nothing moves or changes size on hover. Hover and pressed are told apart by fill colour, border colour and underline only. Chevrons are `clip-path` shapes on an empty `::after`, not rotated borders. A masthead is a normal full-width block, never `100vw`.

## Copy voice

Spanish (`es-ES`), sentence case, *tú* form, plain verbs. A control says where it goes or what it does. Counts use real plurals. Prices read `1.234,50 €` with a non-breaking space before the euro sign. The site name is `{siteName}`, never typed.

## Accessibility floor

Visible 3 px focus ring with a 2 px offset on every interactive element: ink (`--focus-ring`) on light surfaces, amber (`--focus-ring-on-ink`) on the header and footer; the card and home tile rings surround the whole element. Text contrast at least 4.5:1, control borders and the ring at least 3:1. Tap targets at least 44 px (`--tap-target`), including the header link, footer links, pagination links, tiles and every category row. Focus order is reading order, with no positive `tabindex`. No motion, so reduced-motion needs no special case. Amber is never the only cue and never text on a light surface. No cookie banner and no consent UI (guardrail 5).

## Tokens

All tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` `:root`, and nothing else may define a colour: no `*.module.css` writes a hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` value, a `box-shadow`, a `transition`, a `transform` or an `animation` (a static test in `tests/unit` enforces it, comments included).

- Colour: the palette above, including the state colours, `--color-chip-text` and `--color-price`.
- Spacing: `--space-0` to `--space-4`, `--gutter`, `--page-pad-block`, `--card-padding`, `--grid-gap`, `--content-width`, `--tap-target`.
- Radius: `--radius-sm`, `--radius-md`, `--radius-lg`, `--radius-pill`, and the alias `--radius`.
- Focus: `--focus-ring` (`3px solid var(--color-focus)`), `--focus-ring-on-ink` (`3px solid var(--color-accent)`), `--focus-offset` (2px).
- Type: `--font-sans`, `--text-sm`, `--text-md`, `--text-lg`, `--text-xl`, `--text-price`, `--text-title`, `--text-display`, `--leading-tight`, `--leading-snug`, `--leading-body`, `--weight-regular`, `--weight-medium`, `--weight-bold`, `--tracking-tight`.

S11.1 kept every name that existed before and changed the value of `--color-border`, `--color-link` and the target of `--focus-ring`; `--radius` became an alias. Component sizes that are not shared stay constants of their component (the 24rem admin form column, the 48rem admin list column, the 9.5rem tile minimum and 22rem tile cap).
